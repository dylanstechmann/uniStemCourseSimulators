- Independently recalculate representative public buffer, kinetics, electrochemical, coordination, solubility, relaxation-lab and self-assessment work.

- Confirm that the `A` and `B` tokens, potentials and activities remain explicitly fictional, and that the charge calculation is not presented as measured chemical yield.

Confirm that indicator ranges are labelled illustrative and that nothing is presented as a laboratory procedure.
- Review whether the paper-only draft complements the existing synthetic relaxation lab and preserved case; assess notation, accessible alternatives, manual accessibility and likely workload.

## Author-side evidence and its limits

Historical version 0.4.6 records a focused suite of 110 passing tests, including independent recomputation of the redox portfolio and the earlier package snapshot identity. No tests were run for version 0.4.7. For 0.4.8 the General Chemistry II recalculation file recomputes all 72 numeric keys. The eight new keys come from an exact charge-balance solution at each titrant volume rather than the region-by-region shortcuts the reading teaches, and the equivalence volumes are located from the steepest point of the computed curve. A further test checks the values stated in the reading. The full-suite result is in the validation report. These checks come from the same AI-assisted authoring effort. These earlier author-side results do not establish independent chemical review, accessible learner usability, semester workload, student learning or university equivalency. Graded homework, exams and a laboratory sequence remain absent; the project is inactive and ungraded.
