# Genetics & Genomics course review handoff

This handoff targets Genetics & Genomics, package version 0.6.0. The [review snapshot](GENETICS_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md) identifies the exact public files. The package remains partial, unreviewed and formative-only; no reviewer or approval is recorded.

## Review scope

- Review the proposed schedule, prerequisites, objective/outcome associations and quantitative level.
- Independently recalculate representative segregation, pedigree, linkage, gene-regulation, association, expression-normalization, Mendelian-randomization and virtual-lab work.
- Check tetrad-class scoring, Hfr entry-time order, co-transduction interpretation, and the limits of plasmid-complementation claims.
- Check the week-5 ordered-tetrad reading: first- versus second-division classification in four- and eight-spore asci, the single-exchange centromere-distance estimate, the rule-of-three bound for zero counts, and the stated multiple-exchange and two-thirds-ceiling limits.
- Check predictions for lactose and glucose inputs, lacI/operator mutations, and negative versus positive prokaryotic control.
- Check the week-6 project guide and its rehearsal: the restated project counts against the draft table, the worked recap of the lesson's cross, the rehearsal's phase logic, interval and outer-marker fractions, coincidence, interference and Bayes keys, and the wording of the five milestone self-checks. Confirm that the guide and rehearsal do not reveal the project's own results.
- Check the association lab's dataset link, which now resolves to the package-pinned CSV.
- Check the portfolio's parental and double-crossover classification, marker order, adjacent and outer recombination fractions, map distances, expected double crossovers, coefficient of coincidence, interference and Bayes calculation under incomplete penetrance.
- Examine whether finite-sample uncertainty and the stipulated no-phenocopy model are explained at an appropriate level.
- Confirm that linkage is not described as causation, the constructed family calculation is not framed as a personal risk estimate, and the project complements the association lab and variant-follow-up case.
- Review notation, accessible alternatives, manual accessibility and likely weekly workload.

## Author-side evidence and its limits

The earlier focused Genetics suite passed 7 tests for version 0.5.5, including independent recomputation of the mapping and penetrance portfolio and snapshot identity. No test suite was run for version 0.5.6. For version 0.5.8, a dedicated test recomputes every new ordered-tetrad numeric key from the counts in its prompt, and the repository-wide Genetics recalculation table includes the same keys. For version 0.6.0, a dedicated test recomputes every rehearsal key from its CSV by phase logic alone, checks the guide's project table against the draft and the guide's worked recap against the lesson, and confirms that neither the guide nor the rehearsal states the project's own results. Run results are recorded in [VALIDATION_REPORT.md](VALIDATION_REPORT.md). The current content validator, objective-coverage report and assessment-candidate audit are structural author-side evidence; none establishes independent genetics review, accessible learner usability, semester workload, student learning or university equivalency. Graded homework, exams and a multi-session laboratory sequence remain absent; the project draft is inactive, and its optional learner guide is ungraded practice.

## Review record to complete only after review

After a review actually occurs, record reviewer name and qualifications, date, package version and archive digest, artifacts examined, concrete findings and disposition. Keep separate decisions for genetic and mathematical correctness, instructional design, assessment, accessibility, workload and release. Do not infer approval from the package hash or test results.
