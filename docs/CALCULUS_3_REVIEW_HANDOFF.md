# Calculus III course review handoff

This handoff targets Calculus III: Multivariable & Vector Calculus, package version 0.5.0. The [review snapshot](CALCULUS_3_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md) identifies the exact public files. The package remains partial, unreviewed and formative-only; no reviewer or approval is recorded.

## Review scope

- Review the proposed 14-week sequence, level, prerequisites and objective/outcome associations.
- Recalculate representative public keys for gradients, extrema, coordinate changes, line/surface integrals, flux and theorem comparisons. For version 0.4.8, check the planar circle orientation, cylindrical bounds, outward side/top/bottom fluxes, direct-versus-divergence agreement and the open-cap limitation. For version 0.4.9, compare the week-6 least-squares and held-variable reading with MIT OCW 18.02SC Unit 2 Sessions 29 and 42–43; recalculate the normal-equation fits, Hessian determinants, log-linear and direct exponential fits, constrained partial derivatives and triple product.
- Check units, sign conventions, integration bounds, Jacobian factors, boundary orientation, regularity conditions and declared tolerances.
- Review whether the week-14 rectangular flux lab and inactive circular-field project teach distinct skills, use correct domains, and keep mathematical conclusions separate from biological interpretation.
- Check the week-13 project guide and its rehearsal: the restated field, region and diffusivity against the draft, the worked example on a separate field, the construction checks it repeats from the draft, the rehearsal's total, flux and theorem-check keys, and the wording of the five milestone self-checks. Confirm that the boundary-flux lab's dataset link now resolves to the package-pinned CSV.
- Assess the clarity of worked examples, notation, accessible alternatives, plots/tables and likely weekly workload.
- Decide whether the inactive project needs revision or could enter a measured pilot; it is not a scheduled course assessment in this version.

## Author-side evidence and its limits

Historical authoring records describe 91 Calculus III numerical and mathematical checks plus project calculations. No tests were run for version 0.4.8. For 0.4.9 the Calculus III recalculation file recomputes all 72 numeric keys. The eight new keys use centred (mean-deviation) slope formulas instead of the normal equations, a finite-difference Hessian of the squared-error function, and explicit substitution of each constraint followed by numerical differentiation instead of differentials. A further test checks the values stated in the reading, including a golden-section direct exponential fit. The full-suite result is in the validation report. Its author-side evidence is content validation (25 packages, 417 lessons, 2,565 questions, 1,423 cards and 25 cases; zero errors and explicit depth warnings), report consistency and direct recalculation of the new circulation and cylindrical-flux examples. These checks do not establish independent mathematical review, accessible learner usability, semester workload, student learning or university equivalency. There is no graded exam, homework sequence, project submission or course credit. For 0.5.0 a dedicated test recomputes the rehearsal keys, the guide's worked example and the project's published construction checks numerically: a Cartesian midpoint sum for each total, a parameterized boundary sum with central-difference gradients for each outward flux, and a numerical divergence over the disk.
