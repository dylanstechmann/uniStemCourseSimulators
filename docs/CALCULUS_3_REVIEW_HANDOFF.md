- Assess the clarity of worked examples, notation, accessible alternatives, plots/tables and likely weekly workload.
- Decide whether the inactive project needs revision or could enter a measured pilot; it is not a scheduled course assessment in this version.

The eight new keys use centred (mean-deviation) slope formulas instead of the normal equations, a finite-difference Hessian of the squared-error function, and explicit substitution of each constraint followed by numerical differentiation instead of differentials. A further test checks the values stated in the reading, including a golden-section direct exponential fit. The full-suite result is in the validation report. Its author-side evidence is content validation (25 packages, 417 lessons, 2,565 questions, 1,423 cards and 25 cases; zero errors and explicit depth warnings), report consistency and direct recalculation of the new circulation and cylindrical-flux examples. These checks do not establish independent mathematical review, accessible learner usability, semester workload, student learning or university equivalency. There is no graded exam, homework sequence, project submission or course credit. For 0.5.0 a dedicated test recomputes the rehearsal keys, the guide's worked example and the project's published construction checks numerically: a Cartesian midpoint sum for each total, a parameterized boundary sum with central-difference gradients for each outward flux, and a numerical divergence over the disk.
