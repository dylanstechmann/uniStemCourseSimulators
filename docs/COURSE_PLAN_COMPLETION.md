## Work through a plan

1. Start the app using the [README setup](../README.md#run-locally). Create a guest session or account, open a course and enroll. Existing learners should review and accept the current version before opening new material; previous version records remain available.
2. Follow the syllabus's calendar order. Read each lesson, work its example yourself, submit its formative practice and reveal retrieval-card answers after attempting them. Reading completion and card ratings record your own progress.
3. Open each virtual lab from its lesson. Download its package-pinned CSV before doing the calculations. Synthetic values are teaching inputs, not experimental measurements by the owner.
4. In the project block, open the guide, distinguish the separate worked example from the rehearsal and project inputs, and complete the rehearsal practice. Exact counts accept integer notation; continuous calculations specify their precision and units.
5. Open **Case studio**, select each project milestone and save the reasoning and checklist privately. Reload once to confirm persistence. These checklists do not grade prose or certify competence. Finish with the guide's integrated report and limitations statement.
6. For Cell Biology and Biochemistry, also follow the scheduled homework and cumulative review links. The labels explicitly identify open, ungraded practice rather than secure exams.

No physical experiment, clinical action or real robot operation is needed for these paper/data exercises. Compare results against the stated model assumptions; a successful calculation is evidence about that model.

## Final eight routes: instructional scope comparison

The final batch adds eight original guides, separate worked cases, five rehearsal items and five retrieval cards per course, and five private milestone self-checks per course. Each guide integrates existing foundational lessons rather than adding nominal calendar weeks. The following comparison inspects actual local explanations and calculations against official course pages; it does not infer depth from counts or topic labels. Sources were checked on 2026-10-22 and remain link-only; no course assets were imported.
