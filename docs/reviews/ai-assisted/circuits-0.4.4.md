## Scope

The lesson develops affine calibration inversion, first-order propagation for calibration and derived quantities, covariance qualifications, and traceable range/condition reporting. It distinguishes calibration from validation and standard uncertainty from bounds or intervals. Existing questions, keys and objectives are unchanged; all scheduled lessons now have expanded or original instructional text. The single synthetic data lab is not a laboratory sequence.
