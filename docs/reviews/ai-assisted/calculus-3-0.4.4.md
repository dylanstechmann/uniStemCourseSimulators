## Numerical checks

For $\mathbf F=(-y,x)$ on the unit circle, counterclockwise parameterization gives circulation $2\pi$ and clockwise parameterization gives $-2\pi$. Green's circulation integral gives $2\iint_D dA=2\pi$. For $\mathbf F=(x,y)$, the unit-circle outward flux and the divergence integral both equal $2\pi$. For $\mathbf F=(x,y,z)$, divergence 3 over the unit ball gives outward sphere flux $4\pi$. The regression checks these constructed identities and orientation signs, not field behavior between real measurements.
