## Scope

The lesson develops KCL/KVL setup, unit-consistent resistor reduction, open-circuit Thevenin voltage, source deactivation for resistance, dependent-source handling and finite meter loading. It checks a loaded port in both the original and equivalent circuits. Existing questions, keys and objectives are unchanged; compact prototype readings remain in weeks 4, 7 and 12.

## Numerical checks

For a 12 V source, 4 Ω series resistor and 12 Ω shunt resistor, the port has $V_{th}=9$ V and $R_{th}=3$ Ω. A 6 Ω load receives 6 V in either representation. A 10 kΩ source driving a 1 MΩ meter delivers 0.9901 of its unloaded voltage in the simple divider model. Existing public keys remain unchanged.
