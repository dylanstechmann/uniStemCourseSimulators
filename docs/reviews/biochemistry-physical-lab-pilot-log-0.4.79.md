For every run, preserve the unedited readings and identify the interval selected for the operational initial-rate slope (mL O₂/min). Report the selection rule before comparing conditions. Plot individual time courses and replicate-level rates; do not treat time points as extra replicate count. Record whether the blank, replicate spread, timing resolution, temperature control, leakage, and apparatus capacity met the criteria established before the pilot.

Collected gas alone does not verify peroxide depletion: dissolved oxygen, leaks, and gas-transfer delays can reduce the captured volume. If a substrate-conversion threshold is used, attach the locally validated peroxide assay or correction method and its uncertainty. Otherwise, report the slope as an operational gas-evolution rate and leave percentage depletion unverified.
