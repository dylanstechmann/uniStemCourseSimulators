# Geroscience course review handoff

This handoff targets Geroscience & Regenerative Biology, package version 0.6.0. The [review snapshot](GEROSCIENCE_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md) identifies the exact public files. The package remains partial, unreviewed and formative-only; no reviewer or approval is recorded.

## Review scope

- Review the proposed schedule, prerequisites, objective/outcome associations and scientific level.
- Independently check representative senescence, stem-cell, intervention, survival, biomarker-reliability and lifespan-lab examples.
- Check the Week 11 Baker (2016)/Harrison (2009) comparison against both primary papers, including intervention details, the 14%/9% age-at-90%-mortality figures, and the limits on cross-study and human claims.
- Review the new dossier's null-value and confidence-interval interpretation, its distinction between association and randomized comparison, and its limits on surrogate claims.
- Confirm that every dataset and intervention/model label in the dossier is explicitly fictional, that it makes no claim about real interventions or human aging, and that it remains inactive and ungraded.
- Check the week-1 evolutionary-theory reading: the survival and selection-strength calculations under a constant extrinsic hazard, the mutation–selection balance comparison, and the toy pleiotropic allele in high- and low-mortality environments. Confirm that Williams (1957), Hamilton (1966) and Kirkwood (1977) are described fairly for the arguments they are named for, that the comparative patterns are stated qualitatively with their confounders, and that nothing implies a human intervention.
- Assess whether the paper-only dossier complements rather than duplicates the survival-analysis lab and intervention-study-design case; check accessibility and likely workload.
