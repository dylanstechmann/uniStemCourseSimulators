# Wet-lab practice track: layer outlines

Drafted with AI assistance on 2026-10-30 as a planning record for the [wet-lab practice plan](WET_LAB_PRACTICE_PLAN.md). These are outlines, not instruction: no lesson below exists yet unless marked as drafted, and nothing here has been reviewed by a laboratory professional, a biosafety officer or a certifying body. Scope sources are named for each layer; public-domain US federal works may be adapted with attribution, and everything else is link-only.

## Layer 0: `lab-measurement` (built, 0.1.0)

Six lessons: concentration and solution preparation; dilutions and dilution series; measuring volume and checking pipettes; balances and weighing; pH measurement and buffer preparation; spectrophotometry and standard curves. Bench protocols: micropipette gravimetric verification; decimal dilutions and the aerobic plate count.

## Layer 2: `cell-culture-practice` (outline only)

Scope: BMBL Appendix H (human and primate cells); ISSCR *Standards for Human Stem Cell Use in Research* (2023, link only); manufacturer-neutral practice. Candidate open protocols are to be checked one by one for license and parameters.

| Lesson | Topics | Bench protocol (planned) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Aseptic technique | working order in the cabinet, what breaks sterility, media and reagent handling, contamination routes | Aseptic medium change with hidden contamination events |
| 2. Media, supplements and reagent preparation | osmolality and pH of media, serum and serum-free systems, filter sterilization, storage and expiry | Filter-sterilize and QC a supplemented medium |
| 3. Passaging adherent and suspension cells | confluence, dissociation agents, split ratios, seeding density arithmetic, population doublings | Passage an adherent line to a target density |
| 4. Counting cells and viability | hemocytometer geometry, trypan blue, counting rules, Poisson error, automated counters | Hemocytometer count with sampling error |
| 5. Cryopreservation and thawing | cryoprotectants, cooling rate, storage in liquid nitrogen vapour, thaw and recovery | Freeze and thaw with viability outcomes |
| 6. Contamination: bacteria, fungi, mycoplasma and cross-contamination | recognition, mycoplasma testing methods, quarantine, authentication by STR profiling | Diagnose a contaminated culture from clues |
| 7. Human pluripotent stem cells | feeder-free culture on matrix, colony morphology and differentiation, passaging as clumps, karyotype and pluripotency checks (link-only ISSCR reporting standards) | Maintain an hPSC culture without spontaneous differentiation |
| 8. Records and traceability | cell-bank tiers, passage records, lot tracking, labelling | — |

## Layer 3: `molecular-lab-practice` (outline only)

Scope: ASCP BOC MB(ASCP) examination content guideline, molecular techniques and laboratory operations sections (link only; the March 2023 revision was read from an archived copy, and the current revision must be compared before authoring).

| Lesson | Topics | Bench protocol (planned) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Nucleic acid isolation | manual and automated methods, lysis chemistry, column and bead binding | Extract DNA and RNA with yield outcomes |
| 2. Quantity and quality of nucleic acid | A260 conversions, A260/A280 and A260/A230 ratios, fluorometry, integrity numbers | Read a spectrum and decide whether to proceed |
| 3. Gel and capillary electrophoresis | agarose percentage and resolution, ladders, staining, capillary sizing | Run and interpret a gel with hidden errors |
| 4. PCR design and optimization | primer design rules, Tm, annealing, Mg²⁺, controls, gradient PCR | Optimize a failing PCR |
| 5. Real-time PCR and RT-qPCR | efficiency from standard curves, Cq, ΔΔCq, melt curves, reference genes, no-template and no-RT controls | Analyse a qPCR run with contamination clues |
| 6. Sequencing | Sanger chemistry and traces, next-generation library basics, coverage and quality scores | Read a Sanger trace and call a variant |
| 7. Contamination control and one-way workflow | pre- and post-amplification areas, UNG, decontamination, monitoring | Trace the source of amplicon contamination |
| 8. Assay validation and troubleshooting | accuracy, precision, LOD, reportable range, controls and proficiency testing | — |

## Layer 4: `lab-quality-systems` (outline only)

Scope: ASCP MB laboratory-operations section (link only); 21 CFR Part 58 (GLP) and 42 CFR Part 493 (CLIA), both US federal works.

Lessons: documentation and notebooks (ALCOA principles of record integrity); SOP and document control; equipment qualification, calibration and maintenance records; quality control and proficiency testing; deviations, investigations and corrective and preventive action; competency assessment and training records; test-system categories (RUO, ASR, IVD, LDT) and who regulates them.

## Layer 5 and the MB applications package

Protein methods, microscopy and flow cytometry, a CRISPR workflow, organoids, and a cell-therapy manufacturing awareness lesson remain to be scoped one topic at a time. The applications third of the MB outline (infectious disease, oncology, genetic disorders, histocompatibility, identity, engraftment and pharmacogenomics) would follow layer 3 and reuse the genetics package where it can.
