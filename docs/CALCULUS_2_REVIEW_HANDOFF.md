- Confirm that the normalized function is consistently described as a mathematical construct, with no implication of measured biological or mechanical behavior.
- Check the week-13 project guide and its rehearsal: the restated function and polynomial against the draft, the worked example on e^(−2x), the rehearsal's improper-integral, Taylor and certificate keys, and the wording of the five milestone self-checks. Confirm that the guide and rehearsal do not reveal the project's own results, and that the force lab's dataset link now resolves to the package-pinned CSV.
- Review whether the project complements the existing force-work lab and radial case, and assess notation, accessible alternatives, manual accessibility and likely weekly workload.
