# Biochemistry I review handoff

The current public package is Biochemistry I: Proteins, Enzymes & Metabolism 0.5.1. Its [review snapshot](BIOCHEMISTRY_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT.md) pins the 90-file package archive and manifest hashes; the 0.5.0 and 0.4.85 snapshots are archived as [BIOCHEMISTRY_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT-0.5.0.md](BIOCHEMISTRY_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT-0.5.0.md) and [BIOCHEMISTRY_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT-0.4.85.md](BIOCHEMISTRY_REVIEW_SNAPSHOT-0.4.85.md). Version 0.5.1 changes no item, key or schedule: it serves the dataset that the week-4 protein-purification lab links, which was not served before. The course remains partial, unreviewed and formative-only.

This increment makes the components the course plan already laid out usable by learners as open, ungraded practice. Eight homework packets, each with a synthetic CSV dataset and an open practice set, are scheduled in weeks 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12 and 14. Week 7 adds a midterm synthesis reading and a midterm review practice set; week 14 adds a final synthesis reading and a cumulative final review practice set that reuses items from both halves of the course. Neither review set is an exam. Week 5 opens an optional protein-state project guide with a separate rehearsal condition and four open practice items, and five project milestones (weeks 5–8) are Case studio self-checks that are never submitted or scored. The packets, readings, datasets and items are original; they were authored with AI assistance and have not been reviewed by the owner or a qualified biochemist. The scope boundary against MIT OCW 7.05 Spring 2020 is unchanged; see the [official calendar](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-05-general-biochemistry-spring-2020/pages/calendar/).

The 11 public assessment candidates, their prerequisite readings and their private unreviewed key or scoring drafts were not changed, and the candidates remain bound to package 0.4.85. The open practice uses separate packets, datasets and conditions; in particular, the project rehearsal uses a different fictional protein from conditions A and B. No candidate is approved or active. The package has two synthetic-data virtual labs and no physical laboratory sequence. Workload is unmeasured.

The review-evidence register and the workload protocol are empty, version-bound records for 0.5.1; their 0.5.0 and 0.4.84 versions remain archived. The external-review checklist and the response-format inventory are still pinned to 0.4.84 and do not describe the new practice. No reviewer, approval, workload pilot or release decision is recorded. An independently drafted physical-lab candidate remains outside the learner schedule and has no local pilot or approval.
