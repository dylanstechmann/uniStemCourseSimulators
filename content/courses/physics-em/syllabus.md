# Physics: Electricity & Magnetism

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.6.0.** Personal hobby and learning project with substantial AI assistance; no university equivalence or institutional credit.

Original instruction develops field/potential signs, electrostatic boundaries, dielectric energy, circuit loading, RC states, magnetic vectors, magnetic-field sources and dipoles, induction, electrode-interface measurement, AC inference, displacement current, ideal plane-wave energy and coherent two-source interference using declared models. An original text connection map includes a verbal description. Nineteen topic readings and four connected reports accompany a synthetic data lab and the optional project guide.

## Prerequisites

Calculus I; Physics: Mechanics recommended. Manifest relationships remain provisional and establish no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Use field and potential models for charge distributions.
- Analyze resistive-capacitive networks.
- Apply magnetic-force and induction laws.
- Relate circuit measurements to sensor loading and safety limits.
- Apply the Ampère–Maxwell law and Poynting energy flux to ideal time-varying fields and plane waves.
- Analyze coherent electromagnetic-wave interference using path difference, phase and the stated geometric assumptions.
