# Equipment datasheets, containment and fabrication evidence

## Learning objectives

- Structure an equipment datasheet and conceptual pressure-load path without producing a mechanical design or duty rating.
- Audit every wetted component and degradation mechanism, including seals, gaskets and linings, with explicit unresolved states.
- Interpret synthetic fabrication, joint, inspection and nonconformance records through traceability, repair/rerating, MOC and HOLD boundaries.

## Datasheets are interfaces between evidence domains

A fictional equipment datasheet begins with service function, connected stream IDs, design-basis revision and model references. It identifies the equipment class generically—vessel, exchanger, pump, agitator or separator—and records the performance questions that other disciplines must answer. Process information describes the declared envelope categories and variability; mechanical information identifies containment loads and load combinations; materials information identifies every exposed component; controls information identifies instruments and failure-relevant signals; inspection information identifies required records and authorized reviewers.

A datasheet is not a rating merely because it contains numbers. The design team must separate calculated model demands from allowable values established by qualified specialists. Vendor claims require revision, scope and provenance. Interface requirements include nozzles, supports, connected piping, instrumentation, insulation or lining systems, drainage and venting interfaces at a conceptual level only. Detailed dimensions, fabrication settings and acceptance criteria belong to controlled engineering work outside this survey. Any blank material, inspection, safety or acceptance field is unresolved and produces a HOLD.

## Conceptual pressure-containment load paths

Pressure containment is discussed through load paths, not sizing. Internal or external pressure, connected loads, support reactions, thermal effects and occasional loads are potential contributors whose applicability must be established. A conceptual sketch traces load from the containment boundary into shells, heads, nozzles, attachments, supports and foundations. Discontinuities and local stresses matter because a simple membrane intuition does not describe every region. External pressure and vacuum can introduce stability concerns distinct from internal-pressure stress.

No wall thickness, allowable stress, weld efficiency, flange selection or pressure-test instruction is derived here. ASME BPVC Section VIII Division 1 is linked only to its official public catalog record. That record identifies the proprietary standard’s broad design, fabrication, inspection and testing domain; it supplies no rules in this package. Real work requires the applicable current edition, jurisdiction, authorized inspection pathway and qualified engineering review. A generic load-path review cannot establish code compliance or fitness for service.

## All-wetted-component compatibility

“Compatible material” is incomplete unless the component, medium model, time, temperature, contamination, cleaning history, stress state and joint condition are specified. The wetted inventory includes the obvious boundary and the less visible parts: internal fasteners, nozzle inserts, impeller or shaft surfaces, valve trim, instrument diaphragms, seals, gaskets, linings, weld or joint regions, coating edges and repair materials. A lining does not automatically protect a damaged holiday or an exposed edge; an elastomer seal can govern compatibility even when the metal boundary is suitable.

Each component receives its own evidence row and source. Generic corrosion charts cannot resolve a specific joint or contaminated service without expert interpretation. If seals or gasket evidence is missing while the principal shell has a record, overall compatibility remains unresolved, not mostly approved. A synthetic completeness calculation makes this visible: nine component evidence records out of twelve required records gives 9/12×100=75% coverage. The 75% is descriptive only; it cannot be averaged into a pass. The three missing components still force HOLD.

## Degradation mechanisms interact

Corrosion may be uniform or localized, and apparent metal loss does not characterize every mechanism. Erosion can remove protective films or interact with corrosion. Fatigue depends on cyclic range, mean state, geometry, environment and fabrication details. Creep requires time, temperature and stress context and can interact with cyclic damage. Gasket relaxation, lining aging and galvanic or crevice conditions may dominate outside a simple bulk-material comparison. These are review prompts, not predictions.

A declared Miner-style fatigue illustration uses cycle-ratio bookkeeping only. Two synthetic blocks contribute 2000/10000 and 500/2500, so the sum is 0.20+0.20=0.40. This idealized sum is not remaining life, an acceptance result or permission to continue service. It omits sequence, mean-stress, weld, environment and multiaxial effects. Unknown spectra or material data remain unresolved even when the arithmetic is easy.

## Fabrication traveler and joint traceability

A fabrication traveler is an evidence itinerary: approved drawing and datasheet revisions, material records, forming and joining operations, personnel/procedure qualification references, inspection points, nonconformance links and final document handoff. This course supplies no welding parameters or WPS/PQR content. It instead asks whether each fictional joint can be traced from unique ID to location, material/joint configuration, qualified procedure reference, performer qualification record, inspection record and accepted disposition.

If 18 of 24 required joint packages are verified, descriptive trace completeness is 18/24×100=75%. Six unresolved packages prevent completion regardless of the percentage. Repeated references to the same joint record do not create additional joints or independent evidence. A joint-family template may reduce transcription but cannot replace joint-specific identity and status.

## Inspection and NDE record interpretation

An inspection or NDE record is interpreted through its scope and limitations: method, coverage, calibration or reference records, procedure and personnel qualifications, acceptance basis, indications, evaluation, date, equipment identity and reviewer. Detection is not characterization, and an indication is not automatically either a defect or a false call. This package provides no NDE instructions, scan parameters or acceptance thresholds. Learners compare synthetic record fields and determine whether the stated evidence is complete enough for the fictional gate.

Missing coverage is not zero indication. An inaccessible region remains uninspected. A report without equipment ID cannot be borrowed from a similar asset. If six of ten required inspection/nonconformance actions are closed with verification, 60% verified closure is descriptive; the other four remain open or unverified and require HOLD.

## Nonconformance, repair, rerating and MOC

A nonconformance identifies the requirement, observed condition, containment of the affected record or hardware, technical evaluation and authorized disposition. Repair and rerating are not paperwork synonyms for accepting a departure. They can change loads, materials, inspection needs, operating envelopes and future degradation assessments. Management of change (MOC) therefore connects the proposal to design basis, drawings, compatibility, inspection, safety analysis, procedures, training and named approvals. Replacement language cannot hide a changed material, seal, geometry or acceptance basis.

No repair procedure, rerating calculation or return-to-service decision is supplied. Every missing compatibility, inspection, safety or acceptance record means HOLD. Real readiness requires named qualified process/mechanical/materials engineers, authorized inspection and safety reviewers, quality and regulatory roles as applicable, and current authoritative standards.

## Common mistakes

- Auditing only the principal shell and forgetting seals, linings and instrument wetted parts.
- Reading a fatigue sum or trace percentage as approval.
- Treating no recorded indication as proof that an uninspected region is acceptable.
- Closing nonconformance administratively without repair/rerating and MOC review.
