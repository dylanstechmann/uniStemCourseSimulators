# Integrated industry design-review capstone project guide

## Learning objectives

- Integrate design, equipment, operability, safety, scale and governance evidence into a reproducible five-milestone review.
- Recompute public rehearsal NPV, lifecycle and CAPA values and evaluate every G0–G7 gate without averaging away a gap.
- Deliver a zero-point handoff that ends only in bounded synthetic review states and names the real reviewers still required.

## Project brief

The optional capstone is a **zero-point, self-assessed paper design review** of a fictional generic process. Use the original [industry-design-review-rehearsal.csv](/api/v1/courses/chemical-process-design-scale-up/resources/assignments/industry-design-review-rehearsal.csv) for rehearsal, then create a new review dossier using the same generic equipment labels B-01, R-01, S-01, RC-01 and U-01. Do not insert real chemicals, suppliers, facilities or operating values. The result is an educational evidence map, not an industrial design package, process validation, operating approval or commercial release.

The rehearsal contains nine scenarios. R01 through R08 each fail one successive gate G0 through G7; all must be **HOLD / evidence_gaps_remain**. R09 has every fictional field populated and named synthetic role placeholders, so it may be `synthetic_review_complete`. That label means only that the teaching dataset is internally complete. It does not mean ready, validated, compliant, approved or safe.

## Required review conventions

Use the current course reading references and record revision identifiers for every artifact. Separate observation, calculation, assumption, inference and decision. Numeric results require formulas and independently reproducible substitutions. Missing evidence is unresolved, never zero or pass. Reused scenarios and repeated instrument records are correlated and descriptive, not independent replication. No confidence interval or pooled replication claim is allowed.

The project excludes real chemical identifiers and recipes; concentrations and production setpoints; startup/shutdown, bypass/isolation and emergency procedures; waste-transport instructions; wall thickness and weld parameters; WPS/PQR or pressure-test instructions; NDE instructions; relief sizing and dispersion/explosion design; equipment duty ratings; controller downloads; pilot run sheets; regulated batch disposition; process validation and commercial release. A topic can be named only to identify its missing qualified-evidence requirement.

## Milestone 1 — basis, alternatives and model verification (G0–G1)

Create the design basis, requirements/preferences table, role matrix, BFD/PFD prose and stream-table index. Define lifecycle functional unit and boundaries. Compare at least two alternatives using capital/operating scenarios, NPV uncertainty and multiobjective emissions/lifecycle criteria without allowing weights to override constraints.

For rehearsal R02, NPV is −600+200[1−1.1^−3]/0.10 = −102.62960180 cost units. For R04, the alternate arithmetic is −750+250[1−1.08^−4]/0.08 = +78.03171001 cost units. Show that G0 failure in R01 and G1 failure in R02 still force HOLD. Document independent balance and model-domain checks, distinguishing verification from validation.

**Milestone self-check:** all requirements trace to evidence and roles; every model has declared assumptions and domain tests; no converged result is called physically validated.

## Milestone 2 — scale and equipment evidence (G2–G3)

Construct a similarity matrix for mixing, pumping, heat removal, reaction/transport, selectivity, separation and fouling. Identify matched and unmatched groups, scale-down hypotheses, DOE experimental units and measurement uncertainty. Do not turn dimensionless ratios into equipment duties.

Build the all-wetted-component register, including seals, gaskets, linings, instrument diaphragms and joint regions. Link the fabrication traveler and joint IDs to inspection/NDE record interpretation, nonconformance, repair/rerating and MOC. Use conceptual load paths only. R03 fails G2 and R04 fails G3 even when their arithmetic is correct.

**Milestone self-check:** unmatched scale effects remain explicit; every component and joint has traceable evidence or HOLD; no mechanical, fabrication or inspection procedure is invented.

## Milestone 3 — operability, safety and MOC (G4–G5)

For the synthetic PFD, prepare what-if, FMEA and HAZOP evidence prompts and a bow-tie dependency map. Separate basic control, alarms and interlocks. Explain ratio, feedforward and override concepts and the effects of sensor/actuator delay, FOPDT uncertainty and PID saturation. Include SPC, autocorrelation, PAT and deviation/OOS/OOT/CAPA monitoring.

LOPA arithmetic may be reproduced only with its limitations: it cannot assign SIL, tolerable risk or approve an IPL. Identify shared sensors, logic, utilities and human responses. R05 fails G4 and R06 fails G5 regardless of favorable economics. Relief, containment and isolation remain role distinctions with missing-evidence HOLD, never design instructions.

**Milestone self-check:** hazard claims have provenance, barriers have independence evidence, human factors are examined, and all MOC/PSSR and measurement/control gaps remain open.

## Milestone 4 — pilot readiness and transfer (G6–G7)

Create a pilot evidence-readiness matrix, not a run sheet. Identify the authorized human reviews, competency records, measurement readiness and unresolved actions required outside the course. Build a sending/receiving-site transfer index with model versions, equipment evidence, data provenance, deviations and continued-verification ownership.

Lifecycle arithmetic for R05 is 200×5+100×0.2−30=990 emission units; the R07 alternate is 240×4+80×0.25−20=960. These are synthetic inventories under declared boundaries, not measured footprints. For R06, verified CAPA is 8/10×100=80%; for R08 it is 9/15×100=60%. Neither percentage closes an unverified action. R07 fails G6 and R08 fails G7.

**Milestone self-check:** no pilot readiness or transfer claim rests on a template; CAPA effectiveness and receiving-site differences remain visible; continued verification has responsible quality and regulatory roles.

## Milestone 5 — integrated design-review handoff

Assemble a versioned handoff with an executive boundary statement, design basis, calculations, evidence register, G0–G7 gate table, unresolved actions and required real reviewer roster. Include every rehearsal row and show why R01–R08 are HOLD and why R09 is only `synthetic_review_complete`. State explicitly that no `production_approved` state exists in this package.

Rehearse a fifteen-minute fictional review presentation: two minutes for scope, four for model/economics, three for scale/equipment, three for operability/safety and three for gate decisions and limitations. Questions should probe evidence rather than invite operational detail. Preserve a change log showing how each answer modifies the evidence map and whether earlier gates must be reopened.

**Final self-check:** calculations reproduce the CSVs; every source and case link resolves; no correlated records are called independent; every missing compatibility, inspection, safety or acceptance item causes HOLD; no operational, validation or release claim appears.
