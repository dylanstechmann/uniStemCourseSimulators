# Chemical Process Design & Scale-Up

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.1.0.**

This original package is an **explicitly partial advanced university/industry survey**, not a complete or measured course. It offers **eight lesson routes in six thematic modules**: design basis/flowsheets/integration/economics; equipment/containment/fabrication evidence; dynamic operability/control/operations quality; inherently safer design/hazard/barrier learning; similarity/scale-down/technology transfer; then G0–G7 governance with a synthetic evidence lab and optional integrated industry design-review capstone in module six.

The package was developed with substantial AI assistance. It is **partial, unreviewed and formative-only**. Readiness is the **review objective, never an achieved claim**. It confers no credit, competency record, professional qualification, physical validation, standard-specific compliance, process validation or commercial release. All species, equipment, scenarios, organizations and data are original synthetic fictional teaching constructions.

## Prerequisites and planning links

Chemical Engineering Foundations and Signals, Systems & Feedback Control are required. Materials Engineering for Fabrication, Probability, Biostatistics & Experimental Design, and Laboratory Safety and Biosafety are recommended where the curriculum graph permits. These project-planned dependencies are valid and acyclic; they establish no university equivalency or admission eligibility.

The package is appended after Chemical Engineering Foundations in the existing Chemical Process Engineering pathway without changing the ten-pathway count. Signals & Control and the recommended packages retain their own graph positions. Thermodynamics, fluid mechanics and heat/mass transfer remain distinct catalog-only nodes.

## Assessment and study policy

Every route has three lesson objectives, six public formative items and three retrieval cards: **24 objectives, eight outcomes, 48 items worth 56 practice points and 24 cards** overall. Every lesson objective and course outcome has public practice mapping. There are **24 base numeric items and 24 authored numeric variants**, all independently recomputed with misconception, domain and tolerance-boundary tests. No numeric form requires unit entry, significant-figure checking or dimensional checking in the grader, so learners must show units and derivations themselves.

The Case studio contains **six zero-point cases**: one integrative case and five optional capstone milestones. All rubric criteria carry zero points and all cases are self-assessment. Nothing is graded, and there are no protected examinations, deadlines or late penalties.

## Honest boundaries

Reused scenario rows and repeated instrument records are correlated and descriptive, not independent replication. Missing compatibility, inspection, safety or acceptance evidence is always unresolved, never zero or pass, and produces **HOLD**. Arithmetic convergence is not physical validation, equipment rating or achieved readiness. LOPA arithmetic cannot assign SIL, tolerable risk or approve an IPL; FMEA RPN is not probability.

No real chemical identifier, recipe, concentration, startup/shutdown, bypass/isolation, emergency or waste-transport instruction is supplied. No wall thickness, weld parameters, WPS/PQR, pressure-test or NDE instructions, relief sizing, dispersion/explosion design, equipment duty rating, controller download, pilot run sheet, production setpoint, regulated batch disposition, process validation or commercial release is supplied.

R01–R08 in the rehearsal remain `HOLD` / `evidence_gaps_remain` for successive G0–G7 gaps. R09 may be `synthetic_review_complete` only because all fictional teaching fields are present. Neither state means `production_approved`; real readiness requires named qualified engineering, safety, inspection, quality and regulatory reviewers using current authoritative standards.
