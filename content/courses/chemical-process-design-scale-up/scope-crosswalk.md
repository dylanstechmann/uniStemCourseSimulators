## Remaining gaps and human review

The package is not a two-semester MIT replacement or a full industry design curriculum. Detailed thermodynamic/property methods, fluid mechanics, heat/mass transfer, reaction/separation design, mechanical code work, relief/consequence analysis, robust/advanced control, statistics, quality regulation and physical pilot work remain separate qualified-study domains. Real readiness needs current authoritative standards and named process, mechanical/materials, control, safety, inspection, operations, quality and regulatory reviewers. Automated arithmetic, schema and link tests are not that review.
