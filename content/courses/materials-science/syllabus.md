# Materials Science

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.1.0.**

This original package is an **explicitly partial foundational university survey**, not a complete or measured course. It offers **eight lesson routes in six thematic modules**: bonding/structure/material classes and functional properties; crystallography/Miller indices/cubic spacing/Bragg XRD; defects/diffusion/slip/strengthening; binary phases/transformations/process–microstructure links; mechanical/environmental failure; then polymers/ceramics/composites with a synthetic characterization lab and optional selection project in module six.

The package was developed with substantial AI assistance. It is **partial, unreviewed and formative-only**. It confers no credit, competency record, professional qualification, experimental validation, standard-specific compliance or material approval. All named materials, scenarios and data are original synthetic fictional teaching constructions.

## Prerequisites and linked continuation

General Chemistry I is required. Physics: Mechanics and Calculus I are recommended where the curriculum graph permits. These project-planned dependencies establish no university equivalency or admission eligibility. Statics & Mechanics of Materials is a linked prerequisite/continuation for stress, strain, beam and structural-failure derivations intentionally not duplicated here.

## Assessment and study policy

Every route has three lesson objectives, six public formative items and three retrieval cards: **24 objectives, eight outcomes, 48 items worth 56 practice points and 24 cards** overall. Every lesson objective and course outcome has public practice mapping. Numerical practice comprises 24 base answers with authored absolute tolerances and independent arithmetic/misconception tests; there are no numeric authored variants in 0.1.0. None requires unit entry, significant-figure checking or dimensional checking in the grader, so learners must show units and derivations themselves.

The Case studio contains **six zero-point cases**: one integrative case and five optional project milestones. All rubric criteria carry zero points and all cases are self-assessment. Nothing is graded, and there are no protected examinations, deadlines or late penalties.

## Honest boundaries

Characterization uses supplied fictional CSV records only. There is no instrument operating procedure, acquisition setting, specimen-preparation protocol or physical laboratory exercise. Missing evidence remains unknown; one peak, mass-loss percentage or thermal event does not uniquely identify a real material.

Phase and heat-treatment discussion is conceptual. There is no heat-treatment recipe, thermal cycle, fabrication setting or process qualification. Mechanical and corrosion examples do not predict real fatigue life, fracture life, corrosion compatibility or fitness for service. Material selection uses fictional constraints and ordinal inputs only and grants no standard-specific compliance, certification, procurement decision or material approval.
