# Agriculture, Construction and Closed-Loop Habitats (partial interdisciplinary survey)

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.1.0.**

This original package offers **eight lesson routes in six thematic blocks**: agricultural systems and site boundaries; PPFD/DLI/lighting/photoperiod/timers/energy; water and material inventories, climate, sensors and control; plant base-editing evidence; conceptual construction load paths and project dependencies; then closed-loop habitat balances, a synthetic audit lab and an optional integrative project in the sixth block. It is not a measured or complete university course.

The package was developed with substantial AI assistance. It is **partial, unreviewed and formative-only**. It confers no credit, competency record, professional qualification, field validation, construction authorization or life-support approval. All scenarios, questions, readings, worked examples and CSV values are original synthetic teaching constructions.

## Recommended preparation

Genetics & Genomics, Biochemistry I and Physics: Mechanics are recommended for their respective strands. Algebra, unit conversion and basic conservation balances are assumed. These project-planned prerequisites establish no university equivalency or admission eligibility.

## Assessment and study policy

Every route has three lesson objectives, six public formative items and three retrieval cards: **48 items worth 56 practice points and 24 cards** overall. Every lesson objective and every course outcome has a public practice mapping. Numerical practice comprises 24 answers; none requires unit entry, significant-figure checking or dimensional checking in the grader, so learners must show units and derivations in their own work.

The Case studio contains **six zero-point cases**: one integrative case and five optional project milestones. All rubric criteria carry zero points and all cases are self-assessment. Nothing is graded, and there are no protected examinations, deadlines or late penalties.

## Honest boundaries

Plant DNA editing is one evidence-literacy component only. The package covers high-level controls, comparators, corrections, unintended-variant evidence and system limitations. It supplies **no guide/target design, delivery or regeneration steps, sequences or laboratory protocol**. No content from the separate `base-editing-evidence` repository is copied; the registry uses the verified Zong, correction and Jin identifiers directly.

Water and material exercises teach inventory accounting only. They provide no nutrient formulation, concentration target or dosing recipe. Sensor and control examples are evidence exercises and do not authorize automated facility operation.

Construction exercises are limited to conceptual load paths, WBS/dependency arithmetic and human review gates. They provide no real construction instructions, member designs, site-specific calculations or inspection approval. Habitat energy, water and outage exercises make no human-rating, habitability, self-sufficiency or life-support claim. Audit rows may reuse scenario templates and are reported descriptively per scenario, never as independent physical replication.
