# Agriculture, Construction and Closed-Loop Habitats scope crosswalk

This crosswalk covers version 0.1.0, an eight-route partial survey grouped into six blocks. It was compiled with AI assistance on 2026-11-26 from the local readings and public official course-page titles/descriptions. It is an **agent-run comparison**, not scientific, engineering or owner review. Topic alignment establishes no equivalence, credit, endorsement or physical validation.

## Comparator boundaries

[MIT OCW 1.74 Land, Water, Food, and Climate, Fall 2020](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/1-74-land-water-food-and-climate-fall-2020/pages/syllabus) is a graduate reading/discussion seminar whose published calendar addresses food demand, water, land and nutrients, crop yield, environmental effects, smallholders, climate, new technologies, trade, Green Revolutions and agroecology. This package reuses none of its material and covers only selected resource-boundary and evidence-context ideas.

[MIT OCW 1.040 Project Management, Spring 2004](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/1-040-project-management-spring-2004/pages/syllabus/) spans organization, WBS/OBS/CBS, planning, uncertainty, scheduling, monitoring, control and project learning. The local construction lesson and optional project use only conceptual WBS, deterministic dependencies and human gates; they do not reproduce that broader management course.

## Remaining gaps

- Full agricultural science, plant physiology, ecology, food-security analysis and locally appropriate agronomy.
- Nutrient formulation and any operational production prescription; these are deliberately excluded rather than marked as taught.
- Operational plant-editing methods and independent expert assessment of molecular evidence.
- Site-specific civil/structural/electrical engineering, detailed construction management, permitting and inspection.
- Real ECLSS architecture, treatment quality, human factors, reliability analysis, emergency response and human-rated verification.
- Qualified scientific/engineering review, measured workload, physical data collection and any graded assessment.
