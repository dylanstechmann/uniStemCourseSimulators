# General Chemistry I

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.6.0.** This personal hobby and learning project has substantial AI assistance and is not an accredited university course.

Chemical accounting, electronic structure, bonding models, energy and equilibrium are developed through original explanation and synthetic examples, with explicit units, precision and assumptions. In this local sequence, week-12 buffers and the calibration lab establish introductory buffer and measurement ideas; General Chemistry II develops coupled solution speciation, kinetics, redox, coordination and complexation. The two packages divide one MIT 5.111SC comparator and do not claim equivalence to two separate MIT courses.

## Prerequisites

Algebra is required; high-school chemistry is recommended. Manifest knowledge associations remain provisional and establish no university equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Balance reactions and quantify limiting reagents.
- Relate electronic structure, bonding, and molecular geometry to chemical properties.
- Use energy conservation and equilibrium models.
- Check dimensions and significant figures in calculations.
