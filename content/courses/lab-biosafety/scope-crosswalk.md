# Laboratory Safety and Biosafety scope crosswalk

This crosswalk compares the local package, version 0.1.0, with the [ABSA International Certified Biological Safety Professional (CBSP) Examination Content: Domain & Task List](https://absa.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/CBSP_Examination_Content_Domains.pdf) (updated 2026; seven domains, task numbers as published). It was compiled with AI assistance on 2026-10-31 from the task titles only. It is an agent-run comparison, not a review by the owner, a biosafety professional or ABSA. The outline is copyrighted and used here as a scope reference only; nothing is copied beyond domain names and task numbers. Topic alignment establishes no equivalence, eligibility, credit or endorsement. ABSA's credentials also require education and documented biosafety experience.

The CBSP is a credential for biosafety professionals who run institutional programs. This package is written for the people who work at the bench, so the program-management and facility-review tasks of Domains V and VII are mostly outside it by design. Status labels: **covered** means a local reading develops the topic with a worked example and practice; **shallow** means part of it is taught; **absent** means no local instruction.

## Remaining gaps

- Respiratory protection programs, exposure monitoring and tuberculosis guidance.
- The NIH Guidelines and Institutional Biosafety Committee review of recombinant work, which the planned molecular and cell-culture layers need.
- Select agents, import and export permits, animal and plant pathogens, and large-scale biosafety.
- BSL-3 and the animal biosafety levels.
- Review by a qualified biosafety professional, measured workload and any graded assessment.
