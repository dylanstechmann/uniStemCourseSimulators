# Laboratory Safety and Biosafety

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.1.0.**

Assess biological risk, apply Biosafety Level 1 and 2 practices, select and use biological safety cabinets, decontaminate and sterilize with verification, work safely with human materials and hazardous chemicals, and respond to exposures. This is layer 1 of the wet-lab practice track: the safety foundation the cell-culture and molecular layers assume.

This package was written with substantial AI assistance. It has not been reviewed by a biosafety professional or any certifying body, confers no credit, competency record or certification, and does not replace the safety training your employer or institution must provide.

## Prerequisites

Laboratory Measurement and Foundations of Cell and Molecular Biology recommended. This package establishes no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Assess biological risk from agent and procedure hazards and choose controls in hierarchy order.
- Apply BSL-1 and BSL-2 practices and select, use and verify biological safety cabinets.
- Decontaminate, disinfect and sterilize correctly, and verify that the process worked.
- Work safely with human materials and hazardous chemicals, respond to exposures, and recognise when shipping requires trained personnel.

## Optional biosafety plan project (ungraded)

The [project guide](assignments/ipsc-project-biosafety-plan-guide.md) asks for a biosafety plan for deriving iPSCs from donor blood: a ranked risk assessment, the containment level and practices, cabinet checks, a decontamination and waste plan with its calculations, and exposure and shipping procedures. Four rehearsal items sit in week 6, and five Case studio milestones give zero-point self-checks. The scenario and numbers are synthetic.

## Assessment and study policy

Every lesson has public formative items (numeric, single-choice and multiple-select) mapped to its objectives, plus retrieval cards. One zero-point case-studio prompt asks for a risk assessment and control plan. Nothing is graded.

## Current limitations

The schedule is proposed and unmeasured, regulations are paraphrased for teaching, and examples are synthetic. Hand skills (PPE, cabinet work, spill response) need supervised practice. Higher containment levels, animal work, select agents, recombinant-DNA rules and respiratory protection are not taught.
