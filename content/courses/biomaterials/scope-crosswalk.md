# Biomaterials and tissue-engineering scope

This course is a partial, application-focused undergraduate sequence. Its title is defensible because instruction connects material properties and cell-material interactions to scaffold and tissue-engineering applications. “Tissue engineering” here denotes a selected application strand, not comprehensive training in tissue regeneration, fabrication, clinical translation or implant design.

## Local sequence

The core instruction is biomaterial chemistry and architecture, protein adsorption, scaffold mechanics/transport/degradation, host-response evidence and failure interpretation. Selected tissue-engineering applications connect those foundations to cell sources, signals, function and translation. The scope crosswalk does not imply that the current formative examples establish material compatibility, tissue regeneration, clinical performance or device safety. Data and examples remain synthetic; workload and qualified review remain open.
