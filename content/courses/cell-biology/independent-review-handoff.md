## Acceptance and release gates

1. Reviewers document findings against the exact version and digest, including a disposition for each relevant item, source, and assessment component.
2. Authors resolve or explicitly disposition every finding; corrected content receives focused and full validation, and reviewers verify material corrections against the new version.
3. Accessibility review and a measured workload pilot are complete. Any credit, equivalency, or semester claim requires the appropriate institutional decision; the comparator map alone cannot support one.
4. Summative candidates remain inactive until qualified reviewers accept the candidate-specific content and keys, human-scoring procedures are defined, and an authorized operator approves delivery.
5. Course maturity changes only when evidence from real checks and human signoffs meets the repository's documented completion gates.

This document is a review aid. It records no reviewer approval, workload result, institutional decision, or release authorization.
