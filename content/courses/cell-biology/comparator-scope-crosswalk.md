These public materials describe courses from different years, levels, formats, and scopes. The map records topic presence only; it does not compare teaching hours, depth, prerequisites, assessment standards, learner outcomes, credit, or institutional equivalency.

## Explicit scope omissions

The local sequence selects topics for a foundations course and does not claim to cover the full breadth of any comparator. It does not provide full units in cellular respiration/photosynthesis or metabolism; recombinant-DNA methods and genome sequencing; immunology, microbiology, or infectious disease; neurobiology; or cancer biology. Some related ideas may appear as examples, but they are not developed as complete units. The original synthetic labs and assessments also are not substitutes for the MIT courses' problem sets, exams, or instructional materials.

## Source and review limits

The source registry retains the exact official URLs, course dates, link-only rights notes, and mapped lessons. MIT 7.016's Fall 2018 calendar lists a 36-lecture sequence; 7.01SC Fall 2011 organizes its resources into four units; 7.28x Spring 2023 is presented in three parts; 7.06x Spring 2023 is an MITx series based on MIT course 7.06. Those structural facts explain the map, but no conversion of their schedules into local weeks is implied.

All local alignments and stated omissions still need review by a qualified cell-biology instructor. No reviewer has accepted this map, checked every objective against the comparator materials, or established workload or equivalency. The course remains partial, unreviewed, and formative-only.
