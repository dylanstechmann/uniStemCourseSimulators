# Week 8 synthesis: controls, replicates, and bounded claims

This reading applies the evidence framework from [Gene expression, controls and replicate-level reasoning](04c-expression-controls-and-replicate-reasoning.md) across earlier course examples. That lesson defines control roles and measurement layers; this review uses a compact transfer table instead of repeating those definitions. No new experiment, question-bank item, or grade is introduced. The cumulative practice remains public, ungraded review, not a midterm.

| Earlier evidence example | Independent unit and useful comparison | Claim boundary to carry forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 5 fluorescence imaging and fractionation ([imaging lesson](05a-cell-imaging-resolution-and-quantification.md), [fractionation lesson](05b-cell-fractionation-enrichment-and-recovery.md), [lab](../labs/01-fluorescence-image-quantification.md)) | Treat the independently prepared culture as the biological unit; images, fields and technical reads are nested observations. Name the background, calibration or recovery comparison used for the measured signal. | Quantified signal supports a claim about the measured image or fraction under the stated acquisition and recovery assumptions; it does not by itself establish total protein abundance, localization mechanism or function. |
| Week 6 replication and repair ([repair lesson](06b-dna-damage-and-repair-evidence.md), [homework 3](../assignments/homework-03-repair-and-replication.md)) | Keep matched lesion, background and DNA-reference measurements with the culture-level time course; do not count assay wells as new cultures. | A change in the normalized lesion signal supports the stated assay-model conclusion, not a complete repair pathway, mutation frequency or repair flux. |
| Week 7 chromatin and reporter evidence ([occupancy lesson](07b-transcription-occupancy-and-reporter-evidence.md), [homework 4](../assignments/homework-04-reporter-and-chip.md)) | Interpret occupancy and reporter measurements as separate links; retain matched input, construct and independently prepared culture comparisons. | Agreement motivates a regulatory hypothesis but does not alone prove direct binding or endogenous-locus causation. |

For any week 1–7 result, write five short statements: **observation**; **comparison/control**; **independent unit and denominator**; **one remaining alternative**; and **the next measurement that could separate the alternatives**. Use language no stronger than the measured quantity and the design support. Written rehearsal responses remain private and unscored.
