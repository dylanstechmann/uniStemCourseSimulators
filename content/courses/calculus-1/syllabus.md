# Calculus I: Differential and Integral Calculus

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.4.9.** This personal hobby and learning project is developed with substantial AI assistance. It is not an accredited university course.

Limits, derivatives and integrals describe local change and accumulation, with constructed examples in growth, transport, geometry and measurement.

## Prerequisites

Algebra, functions and trigonometry. Structured prerequisite relationships appear in the manifest and remain subject to author review. This package establishes no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Evaluate limits and justify continuity claims.
- Differentiate composite and implicit functions.
- Use derivatives to analyze and optimize models.
- Interpret definite integrals as accumulated change.
- Apply Rolle's and Mean Value Theorems and their consequences to justify monotonicity, uniqueness, and rate bounds.

## Proposed 14-week schedule

This is a proposed sequence, not measured workload or a reviewed semester. Weeks 1, 3, 6 and 9 have expanded foundational readings; week 7 now pairs derivative-sign analysis with a Mean Value Theorem sequence, and week 4 pairs implicit differentiation with inverse, exponential and logarithmic derivatives. Each reading has public formative practice and retrieval cards. Week 13 is a synthetic-data lab, and week 14 prepares the existing washout case checklist.

## Assessment and study policy

There are 16 readings, 113 public practice items and 56 cards, plus the original self-assessment case. Numeric, single-choice, CSV-upload, structured-choice and multiple-select checks support the new material. One preserved symbolic item supports bounded rational expressions only; it is not general symbolic calculus grading. Practice points are feedback, with unlimited retries and no institutional grade, university credit or mastery certification. Written discussion criteria are not automatically graded prose. Assessment outcome lists reflect the objectives their items assess; the prototype links remain provisional.

## Current limitations

No compact prototype reading remains. There is no graded homework sequence, midterm, final or activated integrative project; the washout portfolio is inactive.
