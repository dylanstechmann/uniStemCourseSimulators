# Geroscience & Regenerative Biology

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.7.0.**

Mechanisms linking aging biology to tissue resilience, damage response, senescence, stem-cell function, and regeneration—taught with evidence appraisal and translational caution.

## Prerequisites

Cell biology; Genetics; Biochemistry; Biostatistics recommended. Structured required, recommended, and concurrent relationships appear in the manifest and remain subject to author review. This package establishes no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Distinguish organismal aging mechanisms from correlates.
- Interpret senescence and stem-cell assays with controls.
- Evaluate causal evidence for an intervention.
- Separate lifespan, healthspan, and tissue-function endpoints.

## Assessment and study policy

Current practice inventory: 23 lessons, 105 public practice items, 83 retrieval cards and 6 Case studio prompts, including five project milestones. Counts do not measure semester depth or independently reviewed quality.

Every lesson has formative items (numeric, single-choice, multiple-select, data-interpretation and, in the lab, CSV-upload and structured items) mapped to its objectives, plus retrieval cards for the review queue. Week 13 is a synthetic-data virtual lab and week 14 pairs a study-design lesson with the course case and its self-assessment checklist. These are practice activities with unlimited retries and no institutional grade, university credit or transferable credit. An [inactive, ungraded surrogate-endpoint evidence dossier draft](assessment-specs/surrogate-endpoint-evidence-dossier-draft.md) is provided for instructor review; it is not activated in the course platform. Week 11 also contains an original comparison of two primary mouse intervention studies inside the existing paper-reading lesson. Workload has not been measured.

## Connected seminar coursework (open, ungraded)

A new week-3 foundation develops telomere maintenance, chromosome-end protection, short-end distributions and the limits of culture/deficiency rescue evidence. Three dossiers connect telomere rescue and lineage-versus-population histories (week 6), context/endpoint/target-population survival comparisons (week 11), and lineage-mixture, scar/function and cumulative regeneration evidence (week 14). Ten checkpoints/cards assess selected values and interpretations. Full appraisals, causal diagrams, proofs, code and reports are retained locally and are not graded. Continuous fields request three significant figures; exact division, doubling and model-implied cell counts have no precision penalty. Counts and proposed weeks do not establish semester workload.

## Data and source policy

Every table in the lessons and inactive project draft is explicitly synthetic teaching data. Published studies are cited as link-only references and summarized qualitatively in original wording; no source text, figure, or dataset is reproduced. The lessons and draft give no dosing, treatment, supplement, or clinical-test recommendations.

## Optional surrogate-evidence dossier project (ungraded)

The [surrogate-evidence dossier project guide](assignments/surrogate-evidence-project-guide.md) makes the planned project usable as open practice. Week 14 includes the guide, a separate worked example and five rehearsal items; Five Case studio milestones save private self-check responses without submission or grading. The inactive review draft below remains separate.

## Current limitations

The schedule above is proposed, not measured, and has not been reviewed by a qualified instructor. There are no graded homework sets, midterm, final or active graded project; the evidence dossier is an inactive instructor-review draft. All four compact legacy prototype readings in weeks 1, 5, 8 and 11 now have expanded instructional readings. The selected claims in the Week 11 comparison were checked against their linked primary papers; the 0.7.0 batch also checks selected telomere abstracts, corrects the 2012-versus-2017 rhesus chronology and separates 2002 regeneration discovery from 2010 lineage evidence. Broader references remain link-only and are not audited sentence by sentence. No lesson recommends any intervention, and nothing here is evidence that any intervention slows or reverses human aging. Qualified human review is required before this course could meet the complete-course standard.
