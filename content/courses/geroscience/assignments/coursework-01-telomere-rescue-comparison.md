# Assignment 1: compare telomere-rescue evidence

## The comparison question

Compare the causal reach of a culture experiment that changes telomerase expression with an engineered-deficiency mouse experiment that reactivates it. Use the linked Bodnar 1998 and Jaskelioff 2011 primary records in the companion. Their indexed abstracts support a bounded comparison of model, perturbation and endpoint. Full text was unavailable during this authoring check, so identify any detail that would require reading the complete article rather than inventing it from the title.

Prepare a claim-to-evidence table, a mechanistic diagram, a synthetic calculation and a proposed discriminating observation. Save the complete dossier locally. Browser checkpoints do not evaluate the written comparison or establish a biological conclusion. No intervention, dosing, experimental procedure or personal health recommendation is supplied.

## Part A: match the experimental unit to the claim

For each paper, record the model system, manipulated component, comparator information actually available in the abstract, measured class of endpoint and the strongest limited causal statement. Separate replicative lifespan of cultured cells from lifespan or function of a whole organism. For the mouse study, keep the engineered telomerase-deficient background in the claim. A rescue of a deliberately deficient system can identify a maintenance-dependent mechanism while leaving its relevance to ordinary aging uncertain.

List at least three things that cannot be established from those indexed records alone, such as comprehensive long-term tumor outcomes, every tissue's response or a human clinical effect. Mark them as unexamined, rather than interpreting missing abstract information as proof that a measurement was never performed. A source-access limit and a scientific null result are different kinds of missing evidence.

## Part B: trace mechanisms and alternatives

Draw arrows from the manipulation through enzyme activity, chromosome-end maintenance/protection, damage response, cell fate and functional endpoint. Add at least one alternative path through selection of a subpopulation or a checkpoint change. Specify which observation would support an intermediate step and which would test function. Explain why greater proliferation alone cannot prove that all original damage was repaired or that the resulting cells are functionally appropriate.

Consider a culture in which the average telomere length rises because short-ended cells are preferentially lost. Contrast that with lengthening within tracked lineages. Identify a lineage-aware measurement that could separate the explanations and a limitation of that measurement. The task is about evidentiary logic; it is not a protocol for altering cell maintenance or checkpoints.

## Part C: calculate a distinct synthetic tail model

Track four selected end lengths 3.1, 4.1, 5.1 and 6.1 normalized units. The mean is 4.6 and minimum 3.1. Stipulate arrest when the minimum first reaches or falls below 2.6, shortening of 0.1 unit per lineage division and no other change. Derive the first-integer trigger count, which is five. A stipulated maintenance increment of 0.05 per division changes the net loss to 0.05 and trigger count to ten.

Show both calculations on an explicit integer sequence and verify the inequality just before and at the trigger. Then change the initial minimum while preserving the mean by adjusting another end; calculate the changed trigger count. Label every value as synthetic and distinguish these tracked ends from a complete genome. Equal averages do not imply equal minimums or equal threshold times.

For a completely separate compatible population count, 64 cells become 2,048. Compute five net population doublings and explain why that ratio does not establish five divisions in every lineage. State what assumptions would be necessary to identify net population doubling with the lineage histories used by the end-length toy model. Do not transfer an exact arithmetic count into a universal biological threshold.

Choose one ambiguity from your mechanism diagram and describe, at the level of experimental logic, an additional independent observable and control that would distinguish it. State the experimental unit, selection issue and functional endpoint. Discuss how a result could support the particular maintenance mechanism yet fail to support a broad organismal rejuvenation claim.
