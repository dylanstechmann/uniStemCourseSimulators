## Part A: distinguish discovery from cell-origin evidence

The regeneration reading cites the 2002 zebrafish report for injury-associated heart regeneration and a later 2010 genetic lineage-tracing study for cardiomyocyte contribution. Explain why a tissue-level observation and a cell-origin claim need different measurements. Avoid assigning all later mechanistic evidence to the year of the initial discovery.

Write the minimum evidence needed to distinguish new cardiomyocytes from larger existing cells, DNA replication without completed division, and expansion of another cell type. Then separate cell-origin evidence from restored structure and recovered function. A label in a cell near the wound does not automatically identify a correctly integrated functional replacement cell. Discuss the relevance of an uninjured reference and an injury control without treating any one control as a universal certificate.

## Part B: solve a declared label-mixture model

Let f be the fraction of a defined newly formed, independently identified mature-cell population derived from source lineage A. Before injury, the probability of detecting the label in A is e=0.6. Other contributing lineages have false-positive label probability q=0.1. Stipulate stable label behavior and equal detection in source and descendants. The expected labeled fraction among new mature cells is p=f e+(1−f)q.

If p=0.36, solve f=(p−q)/(e−q)=0.52. Out of a stipulated 1,000 newly formed mature cells, that model implies 520 derived from A. This is a conditional model calculation, not a count directly observed in real tissue. Check that e differs from q and that p lies between them so the inferred fraction is in [0,1]. A label fraction outside that interval would signal inconsistent inputs or failed assumptions, rather than a biologically meaningful negative source contribution.

Compare the naive calculation p/e=0.6, which omits background labeling. Explain why even the corrected 0.52 can fail if labeling changes the fitness of A, if detection differs after differentiation, if another lineage also acquires label, or if fusion transfers the signal. Specify one independent check for each of two chosen failure paths. A model with e=q contains no information about f regardless of sample size.

## Part C: retain structural and functional endpoints

At a stipulated horizon, the injury-control mean scar fraction is 0.24 and an intervention group's is 0.12. The absolute difference is −0.12 and relative reduction is 0.5. A separate normalized functional endpoint is 0.5 in the injury control, 0.7 in the intervention group and 0.9 in an uninjured reference. Improvement relative to injury is 0.2, while a deficit of 0.2 remains relative to the uninjured reference.

Report all those quantities without calling the outcome complete restoration. They also do not establish that the inferred A-derived cells caused the functional change. An intervention could alter inflammation, loading or scar properties through another path. Draw that alternative in your causal diagram and specify what additional evidence would be needed to test the proposed cell-mediated mechanism. Explain why an early DNA-synthesis signal and a later functional measurement cannot be casually merged into one unobserved trajectory.
