# Chemical Engineering Foundations

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.1.0.**

This original package is an **explicitly partial university-level survey**, not a complete or measured course. It offers **eight lesson routes in six thematic modules**: process variables/BFD-PFD/stream tables/units/degrees of freedom/balances; multistream/reactive/recycle systems and reconciliation; energy and simple binary VLE; fluid/heat/mass-transfer concepts; ideal reactors/selectivity/RTD; then separation concepts with a synthetic process-reconciliation lab and optional integrated model-audit project in module six.

The package was developed with substantial AI assistance. It is **partial, unreviewed and formative-only**. It confers no credit, competency record, professional qualification, physical validation, process design or safety/release approval. All species are generic A/B/S and every process, scenario and datum is original synthetic teaching material.

## Prerequisites and deferred distinct packages

General Chemistry II and Calculus II are required. Thermodynamics & Transport in Bioengineering, Probability, Biostatistics & Experimental Design, and Laboratory Measurement and Solution Preparation are recommended where the curriculum graph permits. These project-planned dependencies establish no university equivalency or admission eligibility.

The catalog-only thermodynamics, fluid-mechanics and heat-mass-transfer nodes are **not promoted**. Their deeper full coverage, along with advanced reaction engineering, separations, process control, safety and design, remains deferred to future distinct packages.

## Assessment and study policy

Every route has three lesson objectives, six public formative items and three retrieval cards: **24 objectives, eight outcomes, 48 items worth 56 practice points and 24 cards** overall. Every lesson objective and course outcome has public practice mapping. Numerical practice comprises 24 base answers with authored absolute tolerances and independent arithmetic/misconception tests; there are no numeric authored variants in 0.1.0. None requires unit entry, significant-figure checking or dimensional checking in the grader, so learners must show units and derivations themselves.

The Case studio contains **six zero-point cases**: one integrative case and five optional project milestones. All rubric criteria carry zero points and all cases are self-assessment. Nothing is graded, and there are no protected examinations, deadlines or late penalties.

Rows reused within a lab scenario are **correlated records, not independent replicates**. Reconciliation is deterministic closure practice, not uncertainty-qualified inference. Missing measurements stay blank and unknown, never zero. Learners should report descriptive per-scenario closure rather than pooled-scenario independence claims.

## Honest boundaries

The lab and rehearsal CSVs are synthetic and public. No real production recipe, chemical identifier, hazardous operational condition, plant operating target, controller setting, line or pump sizing, equipment rating or relief sizing is supplied. No safety, release or operating approval is granted. Arithmetic closure is not proof of safe or feasible operation.

The VLE exercise uses declared synthetic K values only and rejects invalid two-phase branches. Fluid, exchanger, reactor and separator results are conditional on reduced models. They do not establish correlation validity, detailed design, scale-up, operability or process safety.
