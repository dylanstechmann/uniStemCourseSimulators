# Signals, systems and feedback-control scope

This is a partial, application-focused undergraduate sequence at the level of introductory linear dynamic systems and single-input/single-output feedback. Its title reflects two strands taught together; it does not claim to replace either comparator course or cover their full scope.

## Where the strands join

The course starts from continuous-time plant models, time and frequency responses, then uses feedback stability and margins to reason about controller choice. Sampling and anti-alias filtering lead into a first-order discrete recurrence and proportional closed-loop pole; the sampled PI lesson and filter-retuning case apply that representation to controller behavior. This is a selected bridge between the two comparators, not a claim of complete discrete-time control design.

The scope crosswalk is a maintainer and learner reference. Existing lesson IDs remain stable; 0.6.0 adds four foundations, four connected reports and twenty-four checkpoints/cards. All examples and data remain synthetic; the course provides no real-device settings, safety procedure or implementation protocol. Workload, qualified instruction review and accessibility review remain open. The comparison is link-only; no MIT text, figures, data or assessments were reused.

## Selected state and implementation extension (0.6.0)

The [16.30 Fall 2010 syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-30-feedback-control-systems-fall-2010/pages/syllabus/) and [lecture topics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-30-feedback-control-systems-fall-2010/pages/lecture-notes/) supply link-only support for a narrow two-state feedback/observer and hidden-mode treatment, plus classical lead and digital connections. Its classical-design/MATLAB/Simulink prerequisites and broader LQR/LQG/nonlinear/robust curriculum are not reproduced. This does not add a third complete source course to the local promise. The exact sampled PI foundation derives one declared old-integrator matrix and its second-order Schur conditions; timing, delay and saturation changes require new equations. The earlier direct-feedthrough recurrence and one-step held plant are explicitly distinguished. Continuous/discrete Fourier examples fix normalization and energy tails without claiming the complete 6.003 transform sequence. All examples are original; no source assessment or software is imported.
