# Materials Engineering for Fabrication

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.1.0.**

This original package is an **explicitly partial advanced university/industry design-review survey**, not a complete or measured course. It offers **eight lesson routes in six thematic modules**: service-driven selection; process–structure–property/defects; comparative fabrication/joining and DFM/DFA; datums/GD&T/fits/stacks; metrology/SPC/capability/yield; then drawing-to-release governance with a synthetic evidence lab and optional release-evidence gap-analysis project in module six.

The package was developed with substantial AI assistance. It is **partial, unreviewed and formative-only**. Fabrication readiness is the **review objective, never an achieved claim**. It confers no credit, competency record, professional qualification, experimental validation, standard-specific compliance, supplier approval or production release. All components, suppliers, scenarios and data are original synthetic fictional teaching constructions.

## Prerequisites and planning links

Materials Science and Statics & Mechanics of Materials are required. Probability, Biostatistics & Experimental Design and Laboratory Measurement and Solution Preparation are recommended where the curriculum graph permits. Chemical Engineering Foundations is an optional acyclic cross-link for process/cure and monitoring context. These project-planned dependencies establish no university equivalency or admission eligibility.

The package joins the existing Mechanical Engineering and Robotics pathway without changing the ten-pathway count. The broader Mechanical Design catalog-only node gains related/recommended links but remains unconsumed and uncompleted by this advanced fabrication review survey.

## Assessment and study policy

Every route has three lesson objectives, six public formative items and three retrieval cards: **24 objectives, eight outcomes, 48 items worth 56 practice points and 24 cards** overall. Every lesson objective and course outcome has public practice mapping. There are **24 base numeric items and 24 authored numeric variants**, all independently recomputed with misconception and tolerance-boundary tests. No numeric form requires unit entry, significant-figure checking or dimensional checking in the grader, so learners must show units and derivations themselves.

The Case studio contains **six zero-point cases**: one integrative case and five optional project milestones. All rubric criteria carry zero points and all cases are self-assessment. Nothing is graded, and there are no protected examinations, deadlines or late penalties.

## Honest boundaries

Worst-case and RSS tolerance stacks remain distinct; RSS requires declared independence. Cp/Cpk is interpreted only as arithmetic for declared stable and appropriately pooled synthetic processes, never for L04’s special-cause record or improperly pooled lots. Repeated gauge measurements are not independent parts.

No feeds/speeds, CNC toolpaths, weld parameters, cure schedules, machine setups, detailed process recipes, standard-specific compliance checklists, supplier approval or production release is supplied. Real readiness needs approved drawings/BOM, qualified engineering/safety review, material/process qualification, calibrated inspection/MSA, lot traceability, nonconformance closure and named authorized human release.

Synthetic cases may conclude only `synthetic_review_complete` when every declared fictional gate has evidence or `evidence_gaps_remain` with a hold. R04 alone satisfies the rehearsal’s fictional gates. Neither disposition means `production_approved`.
