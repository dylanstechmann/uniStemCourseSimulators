## Candidate cases

1. Use the supplied two-step steady-state model and control coefficient. Which pair of observations can coexist at steady state when input and output rates change together? Use the supplied small-change control coefficient to estimate the local fractional flux response to the stated enzyme change; report percent.
2. Use the regulation time-scale inputs in the dataset. Which regulatory layer can change activity rapidly without waiting for new protein synthesis? Calculate the stated first-order response time, in hours, to reach the supplied fraction of the new enzyme level.
3. Use the oxidative and nonoxidative pentose-phosphate pathway data and stated carbon boundary. Calculate the amount of NADPH output, in mmol, from the listed oxidative-branch input. Which statement describes the nonoxidative branch?
4. Use the explicit palmitate synthesis accounting convention and precursor values. Calculate the amount of malonyl-CoA required, in mmol, for the stated palmitate synthesis amount. Which accounting statement is needed when reporting the ATP and NADPH cost?
5. Trace the stipulated citrate-shuttle and acetyl-CoA carboxylase regulation. What role does citrate export play in cytosolic fatty-acid synthesis? What is the pathway consequence of elevated malonyl-CoA in the stated model?
6. Use the glycerolipid and tissue labels in the synthetic data. Which sequence leads from glycerol-3-phosphate toward stored triacylglycerol and its hepatic export route? Which statement distinguishes adipose intracellular lipolysis from capillary lipoprotein lipase?
7. A fictional respiratory-chain perturbation changes ATP demand, substrate uptake and lactate output. Calculate glucose uptake, in mmol, needed for the listed ATP demand when oxidative phosphorylation is blocked and the stated glycolytic yield applies. Calculate the lactate output, in mmol, under the stated glucose-to-lactate carbon balance.
8. Interpret the paired redox and tracer summaries using only the measurement labels provided. What can a changed lactate/pyruvate ratio support in this teaching model? What does tracer labeling add to the interpretation?
9. Choose a follow-up measurement for a fictional oxidative-phosphorylation versus glycolytic defect. Which measurement pair best separates a primary oxidative-phosphorylation impairment from a primary glycolytic defect? How should the follow-up result be reported?
10. Use the synthetic [1,2-13C2]glucose/ribose isotopomer distribution to calculate the labeled-ribose fraction, then state what the pattern can and cannot establish about PPP flux.
