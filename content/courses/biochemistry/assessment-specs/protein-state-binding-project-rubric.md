# Protein-state and ligand-binding project: proposed human-review rubric

**Status: draft, uncalibrated and inactive.** This rubric proposes 100 points for an instructor's future human review. It does not authorize a grade, automatically score prose or replace reviewer judgment. Use only after qualified scientific, assessment, accessibility and fairness review.

For each criterion, assign an anchor from 0–4 and calculate `criterion points = anchor / 4 × criterion weight`. Half-step anchors may be used only if the instructor approves them before assignment.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence to assess |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Charge model | 15 | Correct signs and fractional-charge calculations at both pH values; avoids inferring binding or stability from charge alone. |
| Folding thermodynamics | 20 | Correct denaturant-dependent `ΔG_unfold`, equilibrium ratio, folded fraction and active folded-protein amount for both conditions. |
| Ligand-depletion analysis | 25 | Uses active folded receptor in the quadratic solution; reports complex and both occupancy denominators with units. |
| Approximation and decision | 20 | Compares the free-ligand approximation with the exact result, explains its direction of error and applies the stated 0.70 threshold to total protein. |
| Assumptions and communication | 20 | Identifies at least two consequential model assumptions, explains when coupling is needed and communicates limits without biological overclaim. |

## Performance anchors

- **0 — absent or unusable:** no relevant work, or the response cannot be interpreted.
- **1 — major errors:** the central model or denominator is wrong; the result does not support the requested conclusion.
- **2 — partial:** some correct setup, with material calculation or interpretation gaps.
- **3 — substantially correct:** method and conclusion are sound, with a limited error or omitted explanation.
- **4 — complete:** calculations, units, denominators and evidence limits are clear and consistent with the stated model.

A high total cannot compensate for a safety, accessibility or scientific error that changes the interpretation. Reviewers should leave criterion-level notes and calibrate scoring on sample work before any use. No sample learner submissions or workload data currently exist.
