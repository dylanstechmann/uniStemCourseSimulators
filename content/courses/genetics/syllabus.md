# Genetics & Genomics

**Maturity: partial. Version: 0.5.8.**

Move from Mendelian inheritance and recombination to microbial genetics, gene regulation and experimental inference. Advanced human causal-inference applications are optional extensions.

## Prerequisites

Foundations of Cell & Molecular Biology. Structured required, recommended, and concurrent relationships appear in the manifest and remain subject to author review. This package establishes no university prerequisite equivalency.

## Authored outcomes

- Calculate genotype probabilities under explicit assumptions.
- Interpret linkage and recombination data.
- Distinguish association from causal variant evidence.
- Design a controlled gene-expression experiment.
- Analyze microbial genetic-transfer and complementation evidence.
- Predict prokaryotic gene expression from regulatory genotypes and environmental inputs.

## Proposed 14-week schedule

This is a proposed scope, not a measured or reviewed semester. MIT 7.03 Genetics (Fall 2004) provides a scope comparator, not an equivalency target. The outline groups related units to make room for microbial exchange, regulatory circuits and tetrad analysis; week 13 is a synthetic association lab, while week 14 is an optional advanced human-genetics extension. Every lesson has public formative items and retrieval cards.

## Assessment and study policy

Every lesson has public formative items mapped to its objectives and retrieval cards for the review queue; practice assessments list linked course outcomes. Week 13 is a synthetic-data virtual lab. Week 14 combines two optional human-genetics applications with the course case and its self-assessment checklist. These activities are ungraded practice with unlimited retries and provide no institutional or transferable credit. Workload has not been measured.

## Inactive integrative project draft

The [three-point mapping and penetrance portfolio](assessment-specs/three-point-mapping-and-penetrance-project-draft.md) uses a fictional testcross to connect phase, gene order, map distances and interference with a separate conditional genotype-probability calculation. It is not scheduled, assigned, submitted or graded; the synthetic family model is not a risk estimate for any person.

## Current limitations

The schedule above is proposed, not measured, and has not been reviewed by a qualified instructor. All compact prototype readings now have expanded instruction. There are no graded homework sets, midterm or final; the new project draft is inactive and ungraded. The course now includes focused bacterial-transfer, operon-circuit and tetrad instruction (unordered two-locus classes and single-exchange centromere distance from ordered asci), while broader tetrad genetics, transposition, phage genetics and eukaryotic genome topics remain incomplete; the package is still partial and unreviewed. References are link-only: their records were checked, six abstracts were read for the sentences they support, and one scanned paper was not read in full. All family, population, association and testcross data are synthetic; no lesson gives genetic-counselling or medical advice, and nothing here says anything about any person's risk. Qualified human review is required before this course could meet the complete-course standard.
