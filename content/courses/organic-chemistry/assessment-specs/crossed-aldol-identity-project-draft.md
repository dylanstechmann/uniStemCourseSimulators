## Author calculation key

Propanal's alpha carbon is its `CH2` adjacent to the aldehyde; benzaldehyde has no alpha carbon with an alpha hydrogen at its carbonyl. The stipulated new bond is between the propanal alpha carbon and benzaldehyde carbonyl carbon. The addition product is `O=CH–CH(CH3)–CH(OH)–Ph`.

Addition conserves the combined formula: `C3H6O + C7H6O = C10H12O2`. Its degree of unsaturation is `(2×10+2−12)/2=5`, accounted for by four units from phenyl and one carbonyl. Dehydration removes `H2O`, giving `C10H10O` and six unsaturation units: phenyl, aldehyde carbonyl and one new alkene.

Card A has 12 integrated hydrogens (`5+3+1+2+1`) and formula `C10H12O2`; the hydroxyl and carbonyl constraints fit the beta-hydroxy adduct. Card B has 10 (`5+3+1+1`) and formula `C10H10O`; the missing hydroxyl feature and one vinylic proton fit the dehydrated candidate under the stated observation model. Neither card assigns absolute stereochemistry, and qualitative signal compatibility does not prove unique identity or purity.

The addition product has two stereogenic centers under this connectivity. Dehydration creates an alkene for which E/Z geometry is a separate question. The supplied formulas, integral counts and qualitative bands do not assign either center or the alkene geometry.
